# Stadium Aura Compressor Engine

Modes are Smooth Opto, Fast 76, and Kid670 Vari-Mu. Threshold, ratio, attack, release, makeup, bleed, detector HPF, timing family, and enable are real APVTS controls. Stereo detection is linked.

The meter reports computed gain reduction. Bleed is a phase-aligned dry/compressed blend at the compressor stage. Fixed timing selects musical defaults; Fixed-Manual combines the family default with user timing. Kid670 uses a soft-knee, program-sensitive approximation rather than a circuit claim.
