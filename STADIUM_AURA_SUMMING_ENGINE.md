# Stadium Aura Summing Engine

Console modes are Clean Console, Vintage Desk, Modern Punch, and Tube Console. They alter compensated density and stereo cohesion. Track Count is explicitly a density macro with multipliers 0.25, 0.6, 0.85, 1.0, and 1.15; it is not true multitrack summing.

Transformer, console density, and bus glue are separate real stages. Default settings are intentionally subtle and level-conscious.
